## Important license and architecture contract

The owner specifically requested **free Pexels food photography**. Public [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/legal-pages/license) and [2026 Terms of Service](https://www.pexels.com/terms-of-service/) permit images in commercial websites, apps and menus without license fees, but bar standalone redistribution/sale of substantially unmodified photos and bulk/systematic copying without explicit permission. WAYTA is **not** a stock photo resale service, but its internal searchable shared Food Image Library is a materially different use from a single restaurant menu. The approved earlier curation document flagged Pexels as **blocked without explicit shared-library clearance**. The owner's new Pexels sourcing decision does not by itself establish a provider-specific rights grant. **Do not mislabel images CC0**. Confirm internal multi-tenant embedded use and any constraints with a qualified reviewer/provider before import; avoid offering original-file downloads/stock functionality to tenant users.

The `import:food-images` CLI on `main` accepts **only CC0** and allowlisted source hosts and **cannot import these Pexels License assets**. These files were intentionally prepared *outside* that importer. A rights-aware authorized local file batch intake or Platform Console workflow is needed for DRAFT uploads with metadata, after validating environment backups, current tags and Platform Admin permissions. No direct production database writes. After safe DRAFT import, independent Review/Publish is still required.
