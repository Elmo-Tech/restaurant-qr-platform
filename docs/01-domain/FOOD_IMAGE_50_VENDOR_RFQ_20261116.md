## Critical licensing / legal reply requested

Please state clearly whether you **own** the image copyrights or have authorization to grant all requested rights. Describe any contributors/agency, and provide the actual entity licensing WAYTA.

For each image, quote terms that explicitly allow WAYTA to:
- Store and present the image in a **shared, searchable, platform-operated library** where current and future independent restaurant businesses can choose it for their own WAYTA menus.
- Reuse the same licensed image across **multiple different restaurants/tenants** in a paid SaaS service; no one-client-only or per-restaurant license unless pricing and rights are explicitly described.
- Serve approved images on restaurant menu websites/mobile guest experiences and related promotional pages, with necessary in-platform sublicensing to tenant users.
- Crop/reformat/compress/optimize originals into WebP, generate 1:1 and 4:5 crops, store backups/CDN copies, archive and reuse.
- Use across Egypt, Saudi Arabia, Italy and worldwide as the service expands, on an agreed continuing/perpetual basis without recurring royalties for normal menu impressions.

A stock-photo Standard / Enhanced / Extended license is **not** sufficient evidence for the above. If any requested use is not covered, please identify it explicitly. We will not resell standalone source files as stock downloads. We need written clarity on whether tenant selection from the shared gallery is allowed.

Please confirm any rights expirations, attribution, usage cap, exclusivity, model/property releases, transferability, termination and refund/replacement terms. A signed asset schedule is required.

## Photography / creative specifications

All six categories must achieve **at least 9/10 independently** during WAYTA's human QA: (1) light, (2) food styling, (3) composition in 1:1 and 4:5, (4) art-directed clean background, (5) premium advertising suitability, (6) exact product/tag accuracy. Averages are **not** acceptable substitutes. Verify recipes and raw meat cuts with appropriate food professional.

Deliver originals (JPEG/PNG, high enough source resolution for clean 1600px WebP) plus practical 1:1 and 4:5 safe crops/preview proofs. Provide original dimensions and file hash/source identity, authorship evidence and licensing schedule. No additional graphics/text overlays. WAYTA may reject unsatisfactory shots and request reshoot/replacement under the agreed review policy.
